🛒 Mwila POS & Inventory System

A functional Point of Sale and Inventory Management System developed to help businesses manage products, process sales, monitor stock and generate business reports.

Project Overview

Mwila POS is a desktop business management application developed using Python, Tkinter and SQLite. It provides essential tools for managing daily sales and inventory operations.

System Features

Product Management

Add, edit, delete and manage products.

Barcode Support

Enter and scan product barcodes.

Product Search

Find products using their names or barcodes.

Sales Processing

Process purchases, calculate totals and change.

Inventory Control

Automatically update stock after successful sales.

Receipt Management

Generate, save and print customer receipts.

Daily Sales Reports

View daily business sales performance.

Best-Selling Products

Identify products with the highest sales.

CSV Export

Export sales records for spreadsheet analysis.

Database Storage

Store business records using SQLite.

Technologies Used

Python | Tkinter | SQLite | PyInstaller

Application Status

The application has been packaged as a standalone Windows executable and tested on the development computer.
